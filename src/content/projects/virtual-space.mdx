Virtual Space is a browser-based tool for planning and exploring spaces in 3D.
Draw rooms with precise dimensions, connect multiple floors with stairs, and add
furniture, doors and windows. Change colors, materials and lighting, then walk
through your design in real time.

It also includes a workshop for creating custom 3D models, shared designs with
reader or editor access, and an offline mode for private projects.
